Speaker Guidelines

PyCon Africa 2026

We are glad that you made it to the list of speakers at PyCon Africa 2026. Don’t panic! Whether this is your first talk or your fiftieth, this guide covers everything you need to know about presenting.

Quick checklist — before your talk

  • Upload slides: Upload your presentation before the deadline.
  • Backup your work: Bring a backup of your presentation on a USB drive in PDF format (HTML is also acceptable if tested).
  • Pack adapters: Uganda uses Type G (three-pin UK style) power outlets. Ensure your laptop charger and power cables are compatible.
  • Video/audio: If your talk includes clips, email program@pycon.ug in advance.
  • HDMI connectivity: All rooms use HDMI projectors. Please bring your own HDMI adapter or USB-C hub.

Presenting at the conference

All slots include 5–10 minutes for Q&A. Please do not exceed your allocated time.

  • Short talks: 30 minutes.
  • Regular talks: 45 minutes.
  • Lightning talks: 5 minutes.
  • Tutorials: 2 hours.

Session chairs will be provided for each talk and your session chair will moderate questions to ensure they are captured clearly for the recording.

Room support

In each room, a session chair from the organizing committee will support you. They will assist with setting up the projector, helping with laptop testing, and giving signals to keep you on schedule.

Tech set-up & testing

Slides must be in 16:9 format for our HDMI projectors. Please use the slide deck that will be provided.

We encourage following the W3C guidance on accessible presentations to reach all attendees.

To ensure compatibility, please test your laptop with the session chair during these specific windows:

  • First day morning: 15 minutes before the very first session.
  • Other morning talks: The evening before at 7:00 PM or immediately after the final session in that room.
  • Afternoon talks: During the lunch break (around 1:00 PM or 1:15 PM).

Beginners’ section: tips for success

If you are new to public speaking, these general guidelines can help you prepare:

  • Slide quantity: Aim for approximately 15 slides for a 30-minute talk or 20 slides for a 45-minute talk.
  • Engagement: Use keywords and phrases rather than reading directly from your slides.
  • Rehearsals: Practice your talk multiple times with friends or colleagues to gauge your timing.
  • Voice: Speak significantly louder than you would in normal conversation to ensure clarity for everyone in the room.

Language and conduct

  • Language: The official conference language is English only.
  • Inclusivity: We encourage all speakers to use gender-neutral and inclusive language.
  • Code of conduct: All presenters must adhere to the PyCon Africa and PyCon Uganda code of conduct — see Conduct and Code of Conduct.

Contact us

For any questions, please reach out to the speaker coordination team at program@pycon.ug.
