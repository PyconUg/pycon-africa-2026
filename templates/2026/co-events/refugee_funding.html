Funding for Refugees & Persons of Concern

PyCon Africa 2026 is committed to making the conference accessible to everyone, including refugees and persons of concern who face financial and logistical barriers to participation.

Several funding and support pathways are available to help you attend — from opportunity grants to dedicated community workshops happening alongside the main conference.

Opportunity Grants

PyCon Africa 2026 offers opportunity grants to support attendees who need financial assistance to participate. Grants can cover ticket costs, accommodation, and in some cases travel expenses.

View Opportunity Grants →

Regional Opportunity Grants

The Python Software Society of Uganda (PSSU) runs a separate regional grant programme for participants from East African countries — including Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, South Sudan, and DRC — with priority given to refugees and underserved communities.

This grant covers support for selected individuals who are passionate about Python and tech and want to attend PyCon Africa 2026 but face financial barriers.

Apply for a Regional Opportunity Grant (PSSU) →

PyLadies Arua Refugee Workshop

A free full-day Python and web development workshop organised by PyLadies Kampala, specifically designed for refugees and underserved youth. Participation is free for selected attendees, with priority given to refugee communities.

Learn about the Refugee Workshop →

Who Is Eligible

Refugees and asylum seekers in East Africa

Internally displaced persons

Participants from underserved or low-income communities

Women and underrepresented groups in tech
