About WiDS Uganda

Creating Space for Women in Data

Women in Data Science (WiDS) Uganda is a community that brings together women interested in data, technology and the opportunities created by data-driven work.

The community provides a space for women to learn from one another, share knowledge, build practical skills and connect with people working in different areas of the data ecosystem.

Through workshops, talks, community activities and networking, WiDS Uganda encourages more women to participate, contribute and grow in data-related fields.

WiDS at PyCon Africa 2026

Bringing Women in Data Together

PyCon Africa brings together developers, data practitioners, researchers, students and open-source contributors from across Africa and beyond.

WiDS Uganda will be part of this wider gathering, creating a dedicated space for women interested in data and technology to learn, share and connect.

The program will bring together participants at different stages of their journey—from those beginning to explore data to experienced practitioners looking to share their knowledge and connect with the wider community.

Women in Data, Beyond the Event

Building Community Beyond PyCon Africa

WiDS Uganda is not only about what happens during one event.

The goal is to create opportunities for women to continue learning, sharing knowledge and connecting with others beyond the conference.

Through community activities and partnerships, we hope to continue creating spaces where women can explore data, build practical skills and find support from others on similar journeys.

PyData Uganda

Connecting with the Open-Source Data Community

This year's WiDS Uganda program is being held in partnership with PyData Uganda.

PyData Uganda connects people interested in using Python and other open-source tools for data analysis, machine learning, scientific computing and related applications.

The partnership brings together two communities with a shared interest in learning, open-source technology and creating opportunities for people to engage with data.

Together, WiDS Uganda and PyData Uganda are creating a space where participants can learn from practitioners, discover open-source tools and connect with the wider data community.
