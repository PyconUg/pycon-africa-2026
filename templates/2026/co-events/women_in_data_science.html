Women in Data Science (WiDS) Workshop – PyCon Africa 2026

Welcome

If you are a woman who has ever been curious about data, how numbers tell stories, or how technology is used to solve real problems in the world, this workshop is for you.

We are excited to welcome you to the Women in Data Science (WiDS) Workshop at PyCon Africa 2026, taking place at Speke Resort Munyonyo in Kampala, Uganda.

This is a beginner friendly workshop. You do not need any background in data science or programming. You only need curiosity and willingness to learn.

During this workshop, you will be introduced to the world of data science in a simple and practical way. You will see how data is used in everyday life, from understanding trends in business to solving problems in health, sports, education, and more.

You will also meet other women who are starting their journey in technology and those who are already working in the field. It is a space to learn, connect, and grow together.

What You Will Experience

This is not a lecture where you only listen. It is a hands on and interactive workshop designed to help you understand by doing.

By the end of the day, you will have:

    Understood what data science means in simple terms

    Seen how data is collected and used to make decisions

    Tried simple beginner friendly activities using real examples

    Explored how data is used in real life situations

    Connected with other women who are interested in technology

The goal is not to make you an expert in one day. The goal is to help you understand the basics and feel confident enough to continue learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any experience in data science?

No, you do not need any experience at all. This workshop is designed for complete beginners.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Yes, please bring a laptop if you have one. It will help you take part in the hands on activities.

Who is this workshop for?

This workshop is for any woman who is interested in learning about data science, whether you are a student, a beginner, or already working but new to the field.

Will I be left behind if I have never done anything technical before?

No. Everything will be explained step by step in a very simple way. You will be guided throughout the workshop.

What is the main goal of this workshop?

The goal is to introduce you to data science in a friendly and practical way and help you understand how you can start your journey in this field.
