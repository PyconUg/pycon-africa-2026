Women in Data Science (WiDS) Uganda is bringing women working, learning and exploring data together during PyCon Africa 2026 in Kampala.

The program will create a space for participants to learn from experienced practitioners, explore practical applications of data and technology, share experiences and connect with others working across the data ecosystem.

This year’s WiDS Uganda program is being organized in partnership with PyData Uganda, bringing together the communities around data, Python and open-source technology.

About WiDS Uganda

Creating Space for Women in Data

Women in Data Science (WiDS) Uganda is a community that brings together women interested in data, technology and the opportunities created by data-driven work.

The community provides a space for women to learn from one another, share knowledge, build practical skills and connect with people working in different areas of the data ecosystem.

Through workshops, talks, community activities and networking, WiDS Uganda encourages more women to participate, contribute and grow in data-related fields.

WiDS at PyCon Africa 2026

Bringing Women in Data Together

PyCon Africa brings together developers, data practitioners, researchers, students and open-source contributors from across Africa and beyond.

WiDS Uganda will be part of this wider gathering, creating a dedicated space for women interested in data and technology to learn, share and connect.

The program will bring together participants at different stages of their journey—from those beginning to explore data to experienced practitioners looking to share their knowledge and connect with the wider community.

Speakers & Facilitators

Meet the People Sharing Their Knowledge

Mercy S. Birungi

Automating Data Collection: Web Scraping and APIs 101

With rich expertise in the telecommunications sector, she is passionate and experienced in using data to turn questions into answers and challenges into opportunities. Having worked with large-scale data systems, she understands that every insight has the potential to improve lives, strengthen businesses and shape the future. She believes that when diverse voices are empowered in data and technology, innovation becomes more meaningful and impactful.

Linda Nalweyiso

Machine Learning, Demystified: Build Your First Model

Linda is a data scientist at Stanbic Bank Uganda, where she builds machine learning systems that generate revenue, improve efficiency and manage risk. With over 8 years’ experience in banking, she has worked across the full lifecycle of applied ML: from predictive modelling to collaborating with cross-functional teams through model deployment and adoption. She currently works with the risk analytics team at Stanbic Bank and is passionate about making machine learning practical and accessible to newcomers. Linda holds a master’s in data science from the International University of Applied Sciences and a bachelor’s in statistics from Makerere University.

Lydia Tasia

Reading the Earth: Hands-on Remote Sensing with Python

Tasia is an enthusiastic geospatial data scientist who has spent the last 10 years creating tools and solutions that tackle real-world challenges in GIS, remote sensing and machine learning. She is passionate about using satellite imagery and spatial data to generate insights that are both technically sound and practical for decision-making in agriculture and environmental systems. Her work combines geospatial analysis, programming and machine learning — using tools like QGIS and ArcMap alongside Python, she develops models for biomass measurement, crop mapping and yield estimation. She currently works with One Acre Fund, supporting geospatial and remote sensing projects through analysis, visualization and research into emerging agricultural technologies.

Jael Nantamu

Building Your First Interactive AI App in Python

Jael is the Founder and CEO of TEGU Systems, a Uganda-based tech startup building data infrastructure for African markets. She holds an M.Sc. in Data Analytics Engineering from George Mason University and previously worked at Capital One in the U.S. Her focus is on building responsible, production-ready data and AI systems for real-world impact.

Women in Data, Beyond the Event

Building Community Beyond PyCon Africa

WiDS Uganda is not only about what happens during one event.

The goal is to create opportunities for women to continue learning, sharing knowledge and connecting with others beyond the conference.

Through community activities and partnerships, we hope to continue creating spaces where women can explore data, build practical skills and find support from others on similar journeys.

PyData Uganda

Connecting with the Open-Source Data Community

This year's WiDS Uganda program is being held in partnership with PyData Uganda.

PyData Uganda connects people interested in using Python and other open-source tools for data analysis, machine learning, scientific computing and related applications.

The partnership brings together two communities with a shared interest in learning, open-source technology and creating opportunities for people to engage with data.

Together, WiDS Uganda and PyData Uganda are creating a space where participants can learn from practitioners, discover open-source tools and connect with the wider data community.

Why Join WiDS Uganda?
