Women in Data Science (WiDS) @ PyCon Africa 2026

A programme dedicated to inspiring and connecting women working in data science, machine learning, and AI across the African continent.

WiDS at PyCon Africa 2026 creates an inclusive space for women to showcase their work, build skills, and grow networks within the data science ecosystem — from beginners to experienced practitioners.

Our objective is to increase the visibility, participation, and leadership of women in data science and AI across Africa by providing a dedicated platform within PyCon Africa 2026.

What to Expect

Sessions and activities designed to empower women at every stage of their data science journey.

Keynotes & Talks

Inspiring presentations from women data scientists and AI practitioners across Africa

Hands-on Workshops

Practical sessions on data analysis, machine learning, and Python data tools

Networking & Mentorship

Connect with mentors, peers, and industry leaders in data science

Community Building

Engagement activities to strengthen local WiDS chapters and networks

Who Should Attend

Women in data science and AI (all levels)

Students in statistics, maths, or computing

Researchers and academics

Career switchers entering data fields

Python developers interested in data science

Underrepresented groups in tech

Impact

Greater visibility for women in African data science

Stronger WiDS chapters and cross-border networks

New speakers, mentors, and community leaders
