PyLadies Arua Refugee Python Web Development Workshop

A free, full-day, hands-on session introducing refugees and underserved youth to Python and web development — organised by PyLadies Kampala.

Building on the success of our 2026 bootcamp in Arua — which trained over 100 participants from refugee and university communities — this workshop equips participants with practical skills that can open pathways to employment, freelancing, and continued learning.

Refugee communities in Arua face limited access to digital skills training and opportunities in the tech space. This workshop aims to bridge that gap through project-based, mentor-led learning.
