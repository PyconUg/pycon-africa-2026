About the Program

Creating More Opportunities to Learn

Python Without Borders is a dedicated program at PyCon Africa 2026 for refugees and persons of concern.

The program provides selected participants with an opportunity to learn Python and Django, work alongside coaches and mentors, and connect with the wider Python community.

The workshop builds on the experience of the 8-week Web Development Bootcamp held in Arua in 2025, where PyLadies Kampala worked with refugee communities to introduce participants to Python, Django and web development.

This year, we are taking that learning journey to PyCon Africa.

From Arua to PyCon Africa

This journey started in Arua.

In 2025, PyLadies Kampala, in partnership with CodeStar West Nile and Muni University, organized an 8-week Web Development Bootcamp in Arua.

More than 100 participants took part in the program, including refugees and students.

Throughout the eight weeks, participants learned Python, Django, Git, web development and deployment through practical sessions and project work. They worked with coaches, asked questions, solved problems and built projects that they were able to showcase at the end of the program.

For us, the experience showed the value of creating spaces where refugee communities can access technology learning, mentorship and supportive communities.

Python Without Borders continues from that experience. The program will give selected participants an opportunity to continue their learning and connect with a much wider Python community at PyCon Africa.

The Workshop

Learn. Build. Connect.

This will be a practical workshop where participants learn by doing.

Participants will work alongside coaches and mentors throughout the day, with time to ask questions, work through challenges and apply what they learn.

Python Fundamentals

Participants will work through the basics of Python and strengthen their understanding of programming and problem solving.

Django

Participants will be introduced to Django and learn how Python can be used to build web applications.

Build a Project

Participants will apply what they learn by working on a practical project during the workshop.

Git & GitHub

Participants will learn the basics of version control and how developers use Git and GitHub to manage and collaborate on projects.

Learn Together

Participants will work alongside other refugee learners, coaches and mentors, creating an environment where they can ask questions, share ideas and learn from one another.

Who Is This Program For?

A Workshop for Refugee Learners

Python Without Borders is specifically designed for refugees and persons of concern. The participant group will include:

Selected participants from the previous Arua Web Development Bootcamp

New refugee participants who meet the program's selection criteria

Refugees and persons of concern interested in developing their Python and technology skills

The majority of places will be available to new participants, while returning participants from the Arua bootcamp will also have an opportunity to continue their learning journey and build on the skills they developed during the previous program.

Participants do not need to be experienced programmers. The workshop is designed to provide practical and supportive learning for refugees and persons of concern at different stages of their technology journey.

Why This Program Matters

Creating Access to Opportunity

For many refugees, access to technology education, mentorship, reliable internet, computers and professional networks can be limited.

The Arua bootcamp gave participants an opportunity to learn in a supportive environment and work on practical projects with the help of coaches. Python Without Borders is an opportunity to continue that work.

By bringing participants to PyCon Africa, we are giving them the chance to learn alongside a wider community of developers, meet people working in technology and see what is possible beyond the workshop itself.

We want participants to leave with more than just a day's worth of lessons.

We want them to leave with new skills, new connections and the confidence to keep learning.

Beyond the Workshop

The Learning Doesn't End at PyCon Africa

The workshop is one part of a longer learning journey.

Our hope is that participants will continue practising, building projects, connecting with other developers and taking part in future community activities.

The experience from Arua has shown us the value of continued access to learning and community.

Python Without Borders gives us a chance to build on that experience and create another opportunity for refugee participants to stay connected to technology and the wider Python community.
