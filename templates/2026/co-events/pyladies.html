PyLadiesCon Africa @ PyCon Africa 2026

A dedicated program within PyCon Africa 2026 designed to empower and support women in the Python ecosystem across the continent.

PyLadiesCon Africa creates a safe, inclusive, and inspiring space for women to learn, connect, and grow through technical sessions, mentorship, and community engagement.
