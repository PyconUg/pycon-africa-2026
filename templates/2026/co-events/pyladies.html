Who Should Attend?

If you are ready to expand your technical horizons, you belong at PyLadies Conference Africa. We actively welcome:

Engineers of All Levels: From junior developers solidifying their stack to Senior Full-Stack Architects and Data Scientists.

Tech Aspirants & Career Switchers: Professionals transitioning into technology looking for a structured, supportive community.

Students & Recent Graduates: Ambitious newcomers aiming to bridge the gap between academic theory and production-ready code.

PyLadies Leaders & Members: Ecosystem builders driving local chapter growth across the continent.

Underrepresented Communities: We are fiercely committed to inclusion and actively encourage applications from marginalized groups, including tech enthusiasts in refugee and displaced communities.
