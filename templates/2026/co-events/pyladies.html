Technology becomes more accessible when people have a community around them.

PyLadies Kampala is committed to creating spaces where women can learn without fear of asking questions, share what they know and find people who can support them along the way.

PyLadies Kampala is bringing women in technology together during PyCon Africa 2026 for a day of practical learning, community, mentorship and connection.

The program will create a welcoming space for women to learn from experienced practitioners, explore new areas of technology, share their experiences and connect with other women in the Python and wider technology community.

Whether you are building your skills, exploring a new area of technology or simply looking for a community to learn with, this is a space to come, participate and grow.

A Community Built Around Learning and Connection

PyLadies Kampala is a community dedicated to supporting women in their journey with Python and technology.

Since its establishment in 2018, the community has created spaces for women to learn, share knowledge, build technical skills and connect with others in the technology ecosystem.

Through workshops, open-source activities, community meetups and mentorship, PyLadies Kampala continues to create opportunities for women at different stages of their technology journeys.

Our activities have covered areas including Python, web development, data science, machine learning, open source, cybersecurity and technology careers.

The community is built on a simple idea: people learn better when they have a supportive community around them.

PyCon Africa brings together Python developers and enthusiasts from across the continent and beyond.

The PyLadies program creates a dedicated space within that larger community for women to learn, exchange ideas and build meaningful connections.

The day will combine practical sessions, talks, mentorship and opportunities to meet other women working and learning in technology.

It is also an opportunity for members of the PyLadies community to connect with the wider PyCon Africa community and discover new opportunities to learn, contribute and collaborate.

Speakers & Facilitators

Meet the people leading the sessions. We are bringing together speakers from across the Python and technology community to share practical knowledge, experiences and ideas with participants during PyLadies Africa 2026.

Charity Delmus Alupo

Machine Learning and AI: Responsible Machine Learning and AI That Serves People

Charity Delmus Alupo is a data scientist and AI consultant, serving as Supervisor of Data Science at the Uganda Revenue Authority and supporting AI training and adoption at Brand Help. Her experience spans Africa and Europe, including technology product management in Uganda and data science and machine learning research in Ireland. She brings together research, product development and practical AI adoption to help organizations make better use of their data.

She holds two master’s degrees: an MSc in Information Technology, majoring in Data Science, from Carnegie Mellon University and an MPhil in Machine Learning from University College Dublin, alongside a BSc in Software Engineering from Makerere University. Her interests include responsible AI, fairness, transparency and human-centered technology, with a focus on how AI can serve people and communities.

Session: Machine Learning and AI: Responsible Machine Learning and AI That Serves People

Deborah Aanyu Oduman

Building Thriving Communities with Python and Open Source

Deborah Aanyu Oduman is a computer engineer, CISA-certified IT governance and audit professional, and open-source community builder. She founded OSCA Kampala, Uganda’s first open-source community chapter, and organised the country’s inaugural Open-Source Summit with over 500 attendees. Her open-source journey began as an Outreachy contributor to Mixxx and has grown through mentoring at She Code Africa hackathons and managing Curious Learning’s global volunteer community. Professionally, she has led PCI DSS certification and ICT governance at Pearl Bank Uganda and advised financial institutions at PwC. She speaks regularly on open source and is building a career in AI governance across Africa.

Session: Building Thriving Communities with Python and Open Source

Tabitha Namwone

Securing the Python Ecosystem: Threats, Dependencies, and Defenses

Tabitha Namwone is a Certified Cybersecurity Educator Professional (CCEP) and MSc Cybersecurity candidate at the University of London. She specialises in cybersecurity education, security awareness, and community capacity building, with a strong focus on human-centred security, digital safety, and empowering organisations and vulnerable communities through practical cybersecurity knowledge. She is a founding board member of Women in Cybersecurity Uganda and an active mentor and advocate for inclusive cyber education.

Session: Securing the Python Ecosystem: Threats, Dependencies, and Defenses

Shakira Ndagire Seruwagi

Quality by Design: Testing and Automation for Reliable Software

Shakira Ndagire Seruwagi is a Quality Assurance Engineer and technology advocate with over seven years of experience across software engineering, quality assurance, and open source. She is passionate about building reliable software, advancing modern testing practices, and helping teams deliver better products. Shakira is also a mentor and advocate for women and young people in technology, and enjoys sharing practical knowledge with the wider tech community.

Session: Quality by Design: Testing and Automation for Reliable Software

Our Journey

From Local Meetups to a Wider Community

What started as a local community has grown through the people who continue to show up, share what they know and support one another.

From beginner workshops to open-source contribution sessions, anniversary events and regular meetups, each activity has helped create another opportunity for women to learn and participate in technology.

The PyLadies Conference at PyCon Africa 2026 is another step in that journey.

This time, we are bringing that community into one of Africa’s largest Python gatherings.
