To accept proposals: open a row (click the title), then use Accept & email speaker at the top — or tick rows and choose the action Accept & email speaker (selected proposals), then Go. To waitlist proposals: use Waitlist & email speaker on the change page, or tick rows and choose Waitlist & email speaker (selected proposals), then Go. To reject proposals: use Reject & email speaker on the change page, or tick rows and choose Reject & email speaker (selected proposals), then Go. You can still edit User response in the table; click Save at the bottom after editing. To resend the programme email without changing status, use Resend programme notification on the change page, or tick rows and choose Resend programme notification email (selected), then Go. When delivery succeeds, the Status email column updates to “Sent”. {% endblock %}
