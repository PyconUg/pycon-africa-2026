To accept proposals: open a row (click the title), then use Accept & email speaker at the top — or tick rows and choose the action Accept & email speaker (selected proposals), then Go. To set Waiting list or Rejected, open the proposal and change Status there. You can still edit User response in the table; click Save at the bottom after editing. To resend the programme email without changing status (e.g. proposals already Accepted), tick them and choose Resend programme notification email (selected), then Go. When delivery succeeds, the Status email column updates to “Sent”. {% endblock %}
